# Project Risk & Daily Management

## The problem

Leadership needed a concise signal for the health of active project work. Reviewing individual tasks across the project system was too granular for a recurring management conversation.

## My role

I helped translate detailed task status into a management-level measure that could be understood quickly while remaining tied to the underlying work.

## Metric design

The approach uses the share of open tasks currently identified as overdue or at risk to create a project-risk signal.

That aggregated measure can feed a daily-management dashboard and recurring leadership communication, allowing the team to identify where attention may be needed without manually reviewing every task.

The important work was not simply calculating a percentage. It was defining which tasks belong in the denominator, what constitutes risk, how the metric should behave as work changes, and how the resulting signal should be presented to leadership.

## Development and validation

The reporting logic was implemented with AI-assisted development and validated against the underlying project data before being relied upon operationally.

Automation allows the dashboard inputs to stay current while the team focuses on interpreting and acting on the signal.

## Outcome

Leadership gained a simpler way to discuss project risk using information already present in the work-management system.
